Privacy Policy

Effective November 21, 2026

The short version
  • You don't need an account, and the GradWay website doesn't use cookies.
  • Your campus, classes and settings stay in your own browser. We never receive them.
  • Your location is used only in your browser, and only if you allow it, to show where you are and to get a route.
  • We don't sell or share personal information, and we don't run ad trackers.

This policy covers the GradWay website (the "site"), run by Oakland Software Co. ("we", "us"). The GradWay iPhone app describes its own data practices on its App Store listing.

What stays on your device

The site saves a few things in your browser's local storage so it remembers you between visits. This information is stored only on your device; it isn't sent to us.

  • The campus you chose and whether you're a student or visiting.
  • Classes you add (name, building, days and time).
  • Settings: map style, map details you turned on, walk or drive, and banners you closed.
  • Saved results to make the site faster: places on your campus, recent searches, building details and the weather. These refresh on their own.

You can delete all of it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser's settings.

Your location

The site asks for your location only when you tap a location button or ask for a route, and your browser shows its own permission prompt. Your position is used in your browser to show your dot on the map, measure distances and request a route. To get a route, the start and end points are sent to a routing service (listed below). If you choose "Share my location", the link goes wherever you send it. We don't store your location.

Visit counts

We count visits with GoatCounter, which doesn't use cookies or track you across sites. For each visit it records the page (which campus and which screen), the site that sent you, your browser and screen size, and your country, worked out from your IP address. GoatCounter doesn't store your IP address. We also count a few actions, such as which quick search was tapped, when a route was requested, and when a sponsored listing was shown or tapped, without any personal information. We use these totals to improve GradWay and to tell sponsors how often their listing was seen.

Sponsored listings

Some places are paid listings and are always labeled "Sponsored". Sponsors receive only overall totals of views and taps. We don't give sponsors any information about you.

Children

GradWay is made for college students, prospective students and campus visitors. It isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update it on this page and change the date at the top.
