How this data is collected

  • Anonymous by design. GradWay doesn't use accounts, names, emails or student IDs, and doesn't store IP addresses.
  • No one's path is stored. For a route, GradWay records the destination, distance, walk or drive, and the hour. Not where someone started, and not their location.
  • Places are grouped. Map activity is counted on a grid of about 150 meters (500 feet).
  • Small groups are hidden. Any destination, grid square or search seen fewer than 10 times in the selected dates is left out.
  • People are counted, not tracked. Each device counts once a day, without an identifier that follows it from day to day.
